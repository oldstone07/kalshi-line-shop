How to read it

FanDuel / DraftKings are the live moneylines. The better of the two is in bold; that's the one everything below compares against. The small line under each team is the no-vig fair price.

Set limit at is the price to enter on Kalshi's limit order screen. It's the current bid, so your order waits until someone sells to you.

That's like turns that Kalshi price into normal odds so you can compare. The small line underneath is what you'd get if you just tapped buy instead, fee included.

Verdict comes from gates measured on this season's real Kalshi fills (see Fill check below). Post now: post the limit at the bid. Wait: the price is falling, or kickoff is more than 2 days off, and orders posted then filled at worse moments. Bet the book: the book's price is worth more than a realistic Kalshi limit. Too late: inside 90 minutes, cancel unfilled limits. Tap a row to see each gate and its measured dollar effect.

≈ per $100 if filled is the realistic value of the limit order, judged by Kalshi's price at kickoff: this season's average fill, adjusted by each gate that has held up. It is usually a little negative. Every way of betting a game costs something; the board shows the cheapest.

Never pay more than is your ceiling. At any Kalshi price above it, the sportsbook pays better, so skip Kalshi.

Totals compare the same line only. On Kalshi, Over is Yes on "Over 40.5 points" and Under is No on that same market. If Kalshi has no market at the book's exact number, the card says so and shows no comparison, because a 39.5 over is a different bet from a 38.5 over. A book posting a different line is shown with its line and left out of the comparison.

Prices move. Check the live price in the Kalshi app before you place an order, cancel unfilled orders before inactives (about 90 minutes before kickoff), and assume no maker fee unless Kalshi shows one on the order screen. This page compares prices; it doesn't predict winners.
